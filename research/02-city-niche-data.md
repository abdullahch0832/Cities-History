### D. Same-format clones of HistoryTabOpen (show the format is saturating)
Searching "Entire History of Istanbul" returns **~12 channels in the last 2 months** with the same title format and **<1,500 views each** (Hoodie Wonders, ChronicleX, Timeline Theory, AncientMinds, Cityseed, Legacy Era Studios, Obsidian Ages, UNEARTHED WORLDS…). The same happens for Venice, Baghdad and Berlin. **Conclusion:** the title template alone no longer wins. You need a sharper *angle*, a *distinct visual style* and *consistency*.
